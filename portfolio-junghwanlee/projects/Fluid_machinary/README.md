## 🔧 Fluid Machinery Overview

The system includes three major fluid machines:

- **Compressor** – increases gas pressure by reducing volume
- **Blower** – delivers moderate pressure increase with high flow rate
- **Pump** – transports liquid through mechanical energy transfer
